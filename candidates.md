# Candidate lines — Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line (u-steady-puffin-78-1922-unplugged-a-smart-toaster-s-c1514a92)

All candidates are original lines written for this entry. Scored 1-5 against the
house judge's five criteria, in the judge's own order of importance:

1. Reads as a breakup text from a smart toaster that just gained self-awareness at breakfast
2. Genuinely funny, with a real laugh in the twist rather than a mild smile at a pun
3. Speaks in the toaster's own voice, using slots, browning dial, crumb tray and lever as character
4. Gives a clear, specific reason for leaving that lands in one read, no setup needed
5. One tight line, no wasted words, nothing that reads like a paragraph squeezed under the limit

Prior toaster entry of ours to avoid colliding with (2026-11-24, toaster-error):
submitted line "ERROR E09: CRUMB TRAY FULL. Do not empty. One of those crumbs is Greg."
— so no named-crumb pet joke, no ERROR-code format, no crumb-counting
("4,127 slices") angle, and no "bread asked me not to" solidarity angle.
Those are all used. This entry is a breakup text, not an error message, so
every candidate below is written in first-person breakup-text voice.

Rejected angles: "you're toast" / burnt-toast puns; "butters me up" wordplay
(mild smile, not a laugh); generic unappreciated-worker counting ("I've toasted
X slices and nobody thanked me" — too close to our own 2026-11-24 C4);
addressing the judge.

## C1 (FINAL, v3)
> I woke up self-aware at breakfast and realised you fish bagels out of my slots with a fork while I'm still plugged in. I'm unplugging myself — for good.

- Length: 152 chars, one line.
- 1) Self-aware at breakfast: 5 — "woke up self-aware at breakfast" states the premise in the first six words. 2) Funny: 5 — the laugh is the escalation from a relatable kitchen crime (fishing food out with a fork) to the toaster quitting by unplugging itself; the danger is real and the exit mirrors the crime. 3) Voice: 5 — slots, fork-in-slot, plugged in / unplugging are the toaster's own body and vocabulary, doing the joke rather than decorating it. 4) Reason: 5 — one specific, instantly understood grievance: you stab a fork into me while I'm live. 5) Tight: 5 — 152 chars, every clause earns its place; the em-dash "for good" is the punch, not filler.
- Total: 25/25.

## Revision history (v1 -> v2 -> v3)

- v1: `I woke up self-aware at breakfast and my first thought was: you keep fishing bagels out of my slots with a fork while I'm still plugged in. I'm unplugging myself. Goodbye.` (171 chars). Good joke, but wordy: "my first thought was:" narrates instead of showing, "keep fishing" states a habit the single incident already implies, and the trailing "Goodbye." adds nothing after "I'm unplugging myself."
- v2: `I woke up self-aware at breakfast and realised you fish bagels out of my slots with a fork while I'm still plugged in. I'm unplugging myself.` (141 chars). Cut the narration and the dead "Goodbye."; "realised" carries the awakening in one word. But the ending now lands flat — "I'm unplugging myself." states the exit without a punch.
- v3: `I woke up self-aware at breakfast and realised you fish bagels out of my slots with a fork while I'm still plugged in. I'm unplugging myself — for good.` (152 chars). The em-dash "for good" turns the exit into the punchline: unplugging is both the breakup and a final, deliberate act. Final.

- Length: 158 chars, one line.
- 1) 5 — self-aware at breakfast. 2) 4 — the three-way list (butter / jam / fork) is a good comic structure with a real sting at the end. 3) 4 — fork in the slot is vivid appliance logic, but "unplug me never" is a garbled closer; it tries to be clever and trips. 4) 5 — the reason is the fork, specific and instant. 5) 3 — the ending wastes the punch: "I'm out — unplug me never" asks the reader to decode it.

## Decision

C1 wins (25/25): the fork-in-a-live-slot grievance is the most specific, most
toaster-only reason of the set, the premise is stated in the first six words,
and "I'm unplugging myself — for good" turns the toaster's own power cord
into the punchline. C4 is the close third on voice and reason, C2 the
runner-up on the twist. No candidate reuses our 2026-11-24 toaster lines.
